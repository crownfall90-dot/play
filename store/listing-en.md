## Full Description

Welcome to **Puzzlegrad** — a cozy, strategic tile-placement board game set in a vibrant medieval world!

Place landscape tiles on the board, connect winding roads, fortify castles, enclose winding rivers, and place your meeples wisely to become the greatest ruler of the realm.

### 🌟 Game Features:

• **Timeless Strategy Mechanics**: Match tile edges, complete bustling towns, close roads, and surround chapels and forts to score points.
• **Adaptive AI Opponents**: Three difficulty tiers (Easy, Normal, Hard) that gently adapt to your win streaks and skills.
• **Versatile Game Modes**:
  - **Quick Match**: 30 cards, ~8 minutes — perfect for quick sessions.
  - **Classic**: 50 cards, ~15 minutes — the golden standard for tactical board gaming.
  - **Epic Grand Game**: 100 cards with rare tiles and festive milestone celebrations.
  - **1v1 Timed Duel**: Fast-paced 2-player mode with a 20-second turn timer.
• **Multiplayer & Friends**: Jump into rated matchmaking or host private rooms with custom rules and one-tap invite links.
• **100% Offline Capable**: Play anywhere, anytime without an internet connection — on flights, commutes, or off the grid.
• **Collections & Customization**: Collect figure sets ("Royal Court", "Realm Folk", "Elemental Powers") and unlock ornate avatar frames.
• **Daily & Weekly Quests**: Complete fun daily objectives, open weekly treasure chests, and compete on global leaderboards.
• **Clean & Respectful**: No intrusive interstitial ads or pay-to-win mechanics. Pure tabletop gaming joy.

Build your legendary kingdom tile by tile in Puzzlegrad today!
